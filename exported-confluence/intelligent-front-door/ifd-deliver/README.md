## Purpose of this phase

The Deliver phase applies, tests, embeds or hands over the agreed future-state approach.

This may include a pilot, implementation support, change activity, training, governance, measurement, operational handover or transition into business-as-usual ownership.

The goal is to make sure the work can be implemented safely, measured properly and sustained beyond the project team.

This phase should answer:

**Can we implement, measure and hand over this work safely and effectively?**

---

## Deliver question

**What needs to happen to test, implement, embed or hand over the agreed approach, and how will we know it worked?**

---

## What “ready to complete or hand over” means

Deliver is complete enough to close or transition when we can answer the following questions with reasonable confidence.

| Readiness question | What good looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Do we know what is being delivered? | Scope is clear and agreed |
| Do we know who is affected? | Customer, staff, service owner and channel impacts are visible |
| Do we know who owns the work after delivery? | BAU ownership is confirmed or clearly proposed |
| Do we know how success will be measured? | Measures, baselines and reporting approach are documented |
| Do we know what change support is needed? | Training, communications and adoption needs are visible |
| Do we know the risks and controls? | Privacy, records, accessibility, security and operational risks are managed |
| Do we know what was learned? | Lessons and recommendations are documented |
| Do we know what happens next? | Closure, handover, scale or further work is clear |

## Deliver exit criteria

Deliver can close or transition when:

- delivery scope has been completed or consciously descoped
- success measures have been reviewed
- outcomes and lessons are documented
- risks and issues are resolved or transferred to an owner
- BAU ownership is confirmed or recommended
- content, knowledge, reporting and process artefacts are handed over
- change, training and communications needs are addressed
- recommendations for scale, iteration or closure are documented

---

## Final Deliver outcome

By the end of Deliver, the team should have implemented, tested, embedded or handed over the agreed approach.

Most importantly, we should be able to answer:

**Can we implement, measure and hand over this work safely and effectively?**

If the answer is yes, the work can close, scale or transition to business-as-usual ownership.

If the answer is partial or no, the team should identify what must be resolved, who owns it and what decision is needed next.
