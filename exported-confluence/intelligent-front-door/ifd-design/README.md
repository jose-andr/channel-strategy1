## Purpose of this phase

The Design phase creates the future-state approach, service logic, patterns, artefacts or concepts needed to test and deliver the agreed opportunity.

The goal is to move from an agreed problem and scope into a practical, testable future-state approach.

This phase should answer:

**Do we have a testable future-state approach that is useful, feasible, accessible and aligned to Channel Strategy Y2?**

---

## Design question

**What should the future interaction, pathway or support model look like, and how will it work across channels, teams, data, content and systems?**

---

## What “ready to move forward” means

Design is complete enough to move into Deliver when we can answer the following questions with reasonable confidence.

| Readiness question | What good looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Do we have a clear future-state concept? | The proposed approach is understandable and linked to the defined problem |
| Do we know how the customer experience should change? | The intended customer pathway or interaction is mapped |
| Do we know how staff and service teams are affected? | Operational impacts are visible |
| Do we know which channels are involved? | Channel roles and handoffs are clear |
| Do we know what content, knowledge or data is needed? | Key dependencies are documented |
| Do we know what needs to be tested? | Testable assumptions are clear |
| Do we understand risks and controls? | Privacy, records, accessibility, security and operational risks are visible |
| Do we have enough to pilot, test or hand over? | Delivery requirements and owners are clear enough to proceed |

## Design exit criteria

Design can move into Deliver when:

- the future-state concept is clear and linked to the defined problem
- the customer journey or interaction flow is documented
- channel roles and handoffs are clear
- staff, service team and operational impacts are visible
- content, knowledge and data needs are documented
- key assumptions have a test plan
- risks and compliance considerations are visible
- success measures are ready to be tracked
- there is enough detail to pilot, test, implement or hand over

---

## Final Design outcome

By the end of Design, the team should have a clear, practical and testable future-state approach.

Most importantly, we should be able to answer:

**Do we have a testable future-state approach that is useful, feasible, accessible and aligned to Channel Strategy Y2?**

If the answer is yes, the work can progress into Deliver.

If the answer is partial or no, the team should identify the specific design gaps, risks or decisions that need to be resolved before moving forward.
