# IFD - Define Workshop

## 1.1. Workshop Purpose & Objectives

The primary objective of this **2-hour Define Workshop** is to validate priority customer journeys established during Discovery, deepen understanding of root-cause customer friction points, identify high-value **Intelligent Front Door (IFD)** intervention opportunities, and establish evidence readiness for the upcoming Design phase.

### 1.1.1. Key Objectives:

1. **Validate Baseline Journeys**: Confirm current-state interaction paths for priority service candidates (e.g., Residential Parking Permits, Infringements, Payments, Rates & Property).
2. **Examine Breakdown Points**: Map operational, technical, and informational handovers where customer context or guidance fails.
3. **Formulate Opportunity Statements**: Translate validated customer friction and evidence gaps into structured IFD opportunity candidates for prioritization.

## 1.3. Service Support Modes & Candidate Journeys

### 1.3.1. Core Support Mode Definitions

- **Routine (Transactional)**: High-volume, predictable interactions suitable for automated resolution or seamless self-service (e.g., standard applications, payments, status checks).
- **Guided (Decision Support)**: Scenarios requiring decision trees, eligibility filtering, or interactive navigation assistance prior to submission.
- **Assisted (Specialist Support)**: Complex, exception-based, or sensitive inquiries requiring human agent intervention.

### 1.3.2. Priority Journey Candidates for Define

1. **Residential Parking Permit Applications** (Routine / Guided)
2. **Infringement Reviews & Enquiries** (Guided / Assisted)
3. **Payments & Account Management** (Routine)
4. **Rates & Property Enquiries** (Guided)

### 1.4.1. Diagnostic Signal Overlay (Annotation Badges)

Use these badges during board mapping to pinpoint breakdown types:

- 🔴 **Friction Point**: Web completion failure, form drop-off, or confusing guidance.
- 🔄 **Repeat / Chase**: Re-contact driven by lack of status tracking or clear resolution timelines.
- 🔀 **Handover Boundary**: Unassisted transition across channels (e.g., Web → Phone).
- ⚠️ **Context Risk**: Customer context lost during transfer, forcing repetition.
- ❓ **Evidence Gap**: Journey step relying on assumptions rather than baseline data.
