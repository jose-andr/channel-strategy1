## Purpose of this phase

The Design phase creates the future-state knowledge model, governance approach, taxonomy, workflows, artefacts and testable concepts needed to support consistent answers across customer channels.

The goal is to move from an agreed knowledge problem and scope into a practical, testable approach that can be piloted or handed over.

This phase should answer:

**Do we have a testable future-state knowledge model that is useful, feasible, governed, accessible and aligned to Channel Strategy Y2?**

---

## Design question

**What should the future knowledge model look like, and how will knowledge be created, approved, maintained, reused and governed across channels, teams, content, data and systems?**

---

## Design exit criteria

Design can move into Deliver when:

- the future-state knowledge model is clear and linked to the defined problem
- the minimum viable taxonomy is documented
- knowledge ownership, approval, review and assurance roles are clear
- channel reuse model is documented
- KMS option or hybrid MVP approach is clear enough to pilot
- required knowledge fields, metadata and source references are defined
- AI-readiness controls are documented
- privacy, records, accessibility, information security and data governance considerations are visible
- key assumptions have a test plan or have been tested
- success measures are ready to be tracked
- priority content areas for pilot or implementation are selected
- there is enough detail to pilot, test, implement or hand over

## Final Design outcome

By the end of Design, the team should have a clear, practical and testable future-state knowledge model.

Most importantly, we should be able to answer:

**Do we have a testable future-state knowledge model that is useful, feasible, governed, accessible and aligned to Channel Strategy Y2?**
