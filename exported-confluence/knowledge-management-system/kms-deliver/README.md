## Purpose of this phase

The Deliver phase applies, tests, embeds or hands over the agreed Knowledge Management System approach.

This may include piloting the future-state knowledge model, testing a KMS or hybrid model, applying the minimum viable taxonomy, activating governance, preparing knowledge owners, updating priority knowledge items, measuring quality and transitioning ownership into business-as-usual.

The goal is to make sure the knowledge model can be implemented safely, measured properly and sustained beyond the project team.

This phase should answer:

**Can we implement, measure and hand over the knowledge model safely and sustainably?**

## What “ready to complete or hand over” means

Deliver is complete enough to close or transition when we can answer the following questions with reasonable confidence.

| Readiness question | What good looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Do we know what is being delivered? | The pilot or implementation scope is clear and agreed |
| Do we know which knowledge areas are included? | Priority services, contact reasons or knowledge domains are confirmed |
| Do we know who is affected? | Customer, staff, service owner, Knowledge, Digital, Contact Centre, CRM and channel impacts are visible |
| Do we know who owns knowledge after delivery? | Knowledge owners, approvers and BAU governance roles are confirmed or clearly proposed |
| Do we know how knowledge quality will be measured? | Measures, baselines and reporting approach are documented |
| Do we know what change support is needed? | Training, communications, guidance and adoption needs are visible |

| Do we know whether the model is maintainable? | Service owners and BAU teams can maintain content within the proposed governance model |
| Do we know what was learned? | Lessons and recommendations are documented |
| Do we know what happens next? | Closure, handover, scale, iteration or further work is clear |

## Deliver exit criteria

Deliver can close or transition when:

- delivery scope has been completed or consciously descoped
- pilot knowledge areas and knowledge items are documented
- knowledge owners and approvers are confirmed or proposed
- minimum viable taxonomy has been tested
- knowledge lifecycle workflow has been tested
- channel reuse model has been tested or validated
- AI-readiness controls have been applied where relevant
- success measures have been reviewed
- outcomes and lessons are documented
- risks and issues are resolved or transferred to a named owner
- BAU ownership is confirmed or recommended
- knowledge model, taxonomy, governance, reporting and process artefacts are handed over
- change, training and communications needs are addressed
- recommendations for scale, iteration or closure are documented

---

## Final Deliver outcome

By the end of Deliver, the team should have implemented, tested, embedded or handed over the agreed Knowledge Management System approach.

Most importantly, we should be able to answer:

**Can we implement, measure and hand over the knowledge model safely and sustainably?**

If the answer is yes, the work can close, scale or transition to business-as-usual ownership.

If the answer is partial or no, the team should identify what must be resolved, who owns it and what decision is needed next.
