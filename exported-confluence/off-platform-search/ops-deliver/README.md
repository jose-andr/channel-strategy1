## Purpose of this phase

The Deliver phase applies, tests, embeds or hands over the agreed Off-Platform Search approach.

This may include implementing priority content and metadata changes, improving authoritative source pages, redirecting or retiring outdated indexed content, retesting Google and AI answers, documenting third-party issues, applying risk controls, measuring outcomes and transitioning monitoring into business-as-usual ownership.

The goal is to make sure off-platform search improvements can be implemented safely, measured realistically and sustained beyond the project team.

This phase should answer:

**Can we implement, measure and hand over off-platform search improvements safely, realistically and sustainably?**

---

## Deliver question

**What needs to happen to test, implement, embed or hand over the agreed off-platform search improvements, and how will we know whether findability, accuracy, source quality and risk have improved?**

## Delivery scope

### In scope

| Area | Description |

### Out of scope

| Area | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Guaranteed Google ranking changes | City of Melbourne can influence but cannot guarantee search engine ranking |
| Guaranteed AI answer changes | City of Melbourne can improve source quality but cannot control AI-generated responses |
| Full SEO audit | Delivery focuses on agreed priority search scenarios only |
| Paid search strategy | Paid search is out of scope unless separately approved |
| Full website redesign | Delivery may update priority source pages only |
| Full content rewrite | Delivery may update priority content only |
| Full AI implementation | AI deployment is out of scope; this work focuses on answer risk and source quality |
| Full third-party content control | Third-party sources may be monitored or escalated, but not fully controlled |
| Enterprise content governance redesign | Delivery may recommend governance improvements, not redesign the whole model |

## Deliver exit criteria

Deliver can close or transition when:

- delivery scope has been completed or consciously descoped
- priority search scenarios, terms and AI prompts are documented
- authoritative City sources are confirmed or assigned
- source owners and review approaches are confirmed or recommended
- agreed content, metadata, redirect or legacy content actions are completed or transferred to owners
- Google search scenarios have been retested where feasible
- AI answer scenarios have been retested and limitations documented
- high-risk issues have mitigations, owners or escalation pathways
- third-party source issues are corrected, escalated, monitored or parked with rationale
- accessibility, plain English, privacy, records, legal, information security and AI safety considerations are addressed
- success measures have been reviewed
- outcomes and lessons are documented
- risks and issues are resolved or transferred to a named owner
- BAU ownership, monitoring and governance are confirmed or recommended
- content, source-of-truth, measurement and monitoring artefacts are handed over
- recommendations for scale, iteration or closure are documented

## Final Deliver outcome

By the end of Deliver, the team should have implemented, tested, embedded or handed over the agreed Off-Platform Search approach.

Most importantly, we should be able to answer:

**Can we implement, measure and hand over off-platform search improvements safely, realistically and sustainably?**

If the answer is yes, the work can close, scale or transition to business-as-usual ownership.

If the answer is partial or no, the team should identify what must be resolved, who owns it and what decision is needed next.
