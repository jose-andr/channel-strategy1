## 1.1. Purpose

This page summarises the results of an Off-Platform Search baseline.

It is designed to make patterns, risks and improvement opportunities visible while preserving the underlying assessment dimensions.

The summary should not convert the results into a single numerical score.

The purpose is to understand:

- where customers are receiving strong or weak outcomes;
- which dimensions are creating the most friction;
- where different platforms behave differently;
- whether problems originate in discovery, source selection, interpretation, currency, actionability or source readability;
- where customer consequences are potentially high risk; and
- which improvements are supported by the available evidence.

Detailed evidence remains in the individual test records.

## 1.17. Comparison baseline

This summary becomes a reference point for future test cycles.

When comparing future results:

- retain the six core platform-result dimensions;
- retain their definitions and assessment thresholds;
- compare like customer intents where practical;
- record material changes to test conditions;
- preserve source readability as a separate diagnostic;
- preserve failure layer, High Risk and confidence as overlays;
- avoid retrospective rescoring unless the methodology change is documented; and
- clearly identify where a framework or platform change limits direct comparison.

The purpose of repeated testing is to understand **which dimensions are improving, deteriorating or remaining unresolved**, not to optimise a combined score.
