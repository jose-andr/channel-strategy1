## Purpose of this phase

The Deliver phase applies, tests, embeds or hands over the agreed Information Architecture approach.

This may include implementing priority label changes, updating channel offer information, aligning Contact Us, IVR, Digital Assistant, website and staff-facing pathways, maintaining label-to-code mapping, supporting change activity, measuring outcomes and transitioning ownership into business-as-usual.

The goal is to make sure IA changes can be implemented safely, measured properly and sustained beyond the project team.

This phase should answer:

**Can we implement, measure and hand over the IA changes safely, consistently and sustainably?**

---

## Deliver question

**What needs to happen to test, implement, embed or hand over the agreed IA changes, and how will we know they improved findability, clarity and channel alignment?**

---

## What “ready to complete or hand over” means

Deliver is complete enough to close or transition when we can answer the following questions with reasonable confidence.

| Readiness question | What good looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Do we know what is being delivered? | Priority IA changes, labels, pathways, channel offer updates and mapping artefacts are clear and agreed |
| Do we know who is affected? | Customer, staff, service owner, channel owner, data and reporting impacts are visible |
| Do we know who owns the work after delivery? | BAU ownership for labels, pathways, mapping and governance is confirmed or clearly proposed |
| Do we know how success will be measured? | Measures, baselines and reporting approach are documented |
| Do we know what change support is needed? | Training, communications, guidance and adoption needs are visible |
| Do we know the risks and controls? | Accessibility, records, reporting, data, privacy, security and operational risks are managed |
| Do we know whether IA changes are maintainable? | Channel owners and service owners understand how to keep labels and pathways aligned |
| Do we know what was learned? | Lessons and recommendations are documented |
| Do we know what happens next? | Closure, handover, scale, iteration or further work is clear |

### In scope

| Area | Description |

| Full website redesign | Delivery focuses on agreed priority IA changes only |
| Full IVR redesign | Delivery focuses on agreed priority menu or routing updates only |
| Full chatbot redesign | Delivery focuses on agreed priority intents, labels or pathways only |

## Deliver exit criteria

Deliver can close or transition when:

- delivery scope has been completed or consciously descoped
- priority IA changes, labels, pathways or channel offer updates are documented
- service owner and channel owner validation is complete
- label-to-code mapping is complete for delivered changes
- supporting content, knowledge and prompts are updated or assigned
- accessibility and plain English checks are complete
- success measures have been reviewed
- outcomes and lessons are documented
- risks and issues are resolved or transferred to a named owner
- BAU ownership is confirmed or recommended
- IA standards, mapping, reporting and process artefacts are handed over
- change, training and communications needs are addressed
- recommendations for scale, iteration or closure are documented

---

## Final Deliver outcome

By the end of Deliver, the team should have implemented, tested, embedded or handed over the agreed Information Architecture approach.

Most importantly, we should be able to answer:

**Can we implement, measure and hand over the IA changes safely, consistently and sustainably?**

If the answer is yes, the work can close, scale or transition to business-as-usual ownership.

If the answer is partial or no, the team should identify what must be resolved, who owns it and what decision is needed next.
