## Purpose

This Risk Register tracks the key risks that may affect Channel Strategy Y2.

Channel Strategy Y2 aims to create a practical **Connected Interactions Framework** supported by service information architecture, response modes, routing principles, customer status patterns, minimum data needs and journey-based measurement.

Because the work crosses multiple channels, teams, systems and governance areas, the main risks are not only delivery risks. They include strategic framing, adoption, data quality, privacy, accessibility, technology feasibility and service owner participation.

## Escalation triggers

Escalate a risk when:

| Trigger | Escalate to |
| --- | --- |
| Risk becomes red | Sponsor / CX leadership |
| Risk affects value drops or timeline | Sponsor |
| Risk affects privacy, records, security or data governance | Relevant governance forum |
| Risk affects public-facing commitments or customer expectations | Sponsor and Communications |
| Risk affects accessibility, language access or equity of access | CX leadership and relevant advisory input |
| Risk requires funding, resourcing or FY26/27 commitment | ELT / senior leadership |
| Risk has become an active issue | Working group and sponsor |

## Risk closure criteria

A risk can be closed when:

1. the risk no longer applies
2. the mitigation has reduced the risk to an acceptable level
3. the risk has become an issue and moved to issue management
4. the decision has been made to accept the risk
5. the relevant workstream or phase has ended and no further action is required
